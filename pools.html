Live pools under the saturation point

If k (the protocol parameter stakePoolTargetNum) goes from 500 to 1000, a stake pool saturates at about 38.9 million ADA. Above that point the rewards per ADA go down. The pools below are alive and have less stake than that.

How the list is made

Every day, from the Cardano chain, a pool is listed when it:

  • has stake in the current epoch and is not retired;
  • has a name and a ticker in its registered metadata;
  • made at least one block since 1 January 2026, so a pool that lost stake but still runs is not left out;
  • answers on at least one of its registered relays;
  • has less stake than the saturation point at k = 1000: the ADA outside the reserves, divided by 1000.

A relay that does not answer from here is not proof that a pool is down: some pools only accept known peers. Those pools are not on this list. The list is made by Smit Blockchain Operations, operator of pool BKIND, which is on it by the same rules as every other pool.
